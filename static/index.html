A quick guide to the AQI

The US Air Quality Index turns pollution readings into a 0–500 scale. Lower usually means cleaner air.

Good0–50 · little concern
Moderate51–100 · usually okay
Sensitive groups101–150 · take care
Unhealthy151–200 · limit exertion
Very unhealthy201–300 · reduce exposure
Hazardous301+ · follow local advice

Meet the pollutants

Different pollutants come from different sources. These readings help explain what may be affecting the air.

PM2.5 & PM10

Tiny particles from dust, smoke, traffic, and fires. Smaller PM2.5 can travel deeper into the lungs.

Ozone (O₃)

Ground-level ozone can form on sunny days when other air pollutants react.

Nitrogen dioxide (NO₂)

A gas often linked to traffic and fuel burning. Busy roads can be a source.

CO & SO₂

Gases associated with burning fuels, industry, and some natural events.
